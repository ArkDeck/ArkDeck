## Equivalence and ownership checks

- All four old upload families lower to `artifact.import.*` with typed kind. HAP signature/module selection, native-library ELF metadata, exact workspace-patch paths and Flash archive validation remain in `RuntimeImportControlHandler`. App XPC allows only HAP, native-library and Flash; the gateway refuses CLI-owned uploads and workspace patches. `DurableImportContractTests.testAppAndCLIUseTheSameTypedImportWithoutSharingUploadOwnership` drives the same daemon through real CLI and production App upload serialization.
- `device.observations` publishes an owner-minted identity/generation with independent USB continuity; direct device information and successful Job facts decorate the App rows without creating binding authority. `DeviceCandidatesContractTests` and `ObserveDeviceSkeletonContractTests` cover the current path, including exact adoption before fixture process execution.
- Job/Artifact consumers follow immutable page cursors. `JobReadResourcesContractTests.testLongUnicodeTimelineIsReferenceAndLosslessBoundedPages` drives the real CLI and production App page reader against the same daemon; `RuntimeAppReadResourcesContractTests` checks malformed and incomplete pages. Job detail reconstructs paged timeline fragments locally; the wire never emits the retired untagged status/array shapes. Artifact readers check tagged owner, immutable digest and exact range counts. Device controls use these readers. `ArtifactResourcesContractTests.testDeviceProductionReaderAndCLIReadTheSameDaemonScreenshot` pairs the production Device provider and real CLI against one daemon; `DeviceProductionProviderContractTests` covers recording pages, gesture timelines, unknown outcomes and malformed ranges.
- `ControlProtocolContractTests`, `AgentClientDeadlineContractTests` and `AgentXPCTransportContractTests` cover current framing, mixed-build refusal, same-connection health, bounds and ownership gates. `CLIControlFailureMappingContractTests.testEveryDaemonMethodIsClassified` checks all 96 retained names. Retired names receive unknownMethod, rather than exposing capability administration.
- Historical durable records, evidence schemas, CLI spelling lifecycles and external ABI versions retain their separate SVC-002/003/004 scope. Raw Artifact bytes and historical hardware evidence are unchanged.
