# The TASK-AIN-011 → 016 chain, measured against the current product (2026-08-07)

TASK-AIN-010P was retired because its delivery vehicle had been deleted and its output already
existed. It is the head of a blocked chain — 011 → 012/013/014 → 015 → 016 — so this checks
whether the rest of that chain is in the same condition. It is, and more completely than 010P
was.

No status is changed here. This is a measurement for the maintainer's decision, host-only, with
zero dispatch of any kind.

## What this means, and what it does not

Every task in the chain is either built on a plane the repository deleted, or delivered by the
engine lane, or both. That is the same shape as 010P, at six times the size — which is expected:
these tasks were written for the v1 device-operation plane, and T25/W3 removed that plane after
they were blocked, so their text froze while the product moved.

What it does **not** mean: that the capabilities are unverified. They are verified — by published
operations, contract suites and passing device journeys — just not through the vehicle these task
blocks describe. Retiring them would remove stale plans, not coverage.

One caveat worth stating rather than glossing: 012, 013 and 016 are worded generically enough
that nothing in their text is provably false today. They are overtaken by delivery rather than
broken, which is a weaker reason to retire and a better reason to re-point.

## For the maintainer

Three coherent dispositions, not a recommendation this document is entitled to make on its own:

1. **Retire 011, 014 and 015** on the same grounds as 010P — each names a deleted component or a
   deleted authority — and re-point or retire 012/013/016 with them, since their only remaining
   purpose was to sit behind 011.
2. **Re-base the chain** onto the engine lane in one scope revision, keeping the task IDs and
   rewriting reachability, authority and seam text.
3. **Leave it** until `CHG-2026-056` r5 is decided, since a core-major change to the authority
   plane will rewrite the E2 references in 014 regardless.

Sequencing note: (3) is not free. Every day these blocks stay as written, they read as outstanding
work to anyone planning a device window — which is exactly what cost the 010P audits three rounds.
